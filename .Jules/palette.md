## 2024-10-04 - Form Label Accessibility and Missing Programmatic Associations
**Learning:** Found a common pattern in the app where `<label>` tags lacked `htmlFor` attributes to explicitly associate with corresponding `id`s on `<input>` and `<textarea>` elements, relying incorrectly on structural proximity for screen readers. Furthermore, some form designs (like RentModal) intentionally omitted visible labels in favor of placeholders due to visual constraints, leading to completely unlabeled inputs for screen readers. Emojis used for success states were also lacking proper semantics (`role="img"` and `aria-label`).
**Action:** When working on form accessibility, explicitly link all visible `<label>`s with matching `htmlFor` and `id` attributes. If visual design explicitly prevents visible labels, ensure `aria-label` is applied directly to the input to fulfill accessibility requirements without altering the established visual layout or relying on custom CSS. Always ensure decorative or informative emojis used in UI states have appropriate roles and aria-labels.
