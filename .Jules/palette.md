## 2026-10-23 - Form Accessibility Constraints\n**Learning:** When visible labels are omitted due to visual design constraints (as seen in RentModal.jsx), adding an aria-label directly to the input/textarea is the required approach to maintain accessibility without violating the 'no custom CSS' rule (e.g., avoiding visually-hidden utility classes).\n**Action:** Always verify if a form lacks visible labels. If so, apply aria-labels; if visible labels exist (like in Contact.jsx), explicitly associate them using htmlFor and id to maximize screen reader compatibility and click hit area.
