## 2024-05-24 - Form Accessibility and Visual Constraints\n**Learning:** When strict design constraints prevent the use of explicit visible `<label>` elements alongside form inputs, relying solely on placeholders degrades accessibility for screen readers.\n**Action:** Use `aria-label` attributes directly on inputs and textareas when visible labels are prohibited by design, ensuring accessibility without violating visual constraints. Conversely, when labels are visible, always use explicit `htmlFor` and `id` bindings.
