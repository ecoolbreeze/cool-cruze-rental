## 2024-05-15 - Form Accessibility Strategy
**Learning:** When structural design constraints prevent adding explicitly visible `<label>` tags next to form controls (like in the `RentModal` placeholders-only design), using structural proximity or omitting labels altogether creates poor screen reader experiences. `aria-label` attributes provide a lightweight, custom CSS-free solution that maintains visual fidelity while fulfilling accessibility requirements. For forms with existing visible labels (like the `Contact` page), implicit structural wrapping isn’t foolproof for all screen readers, so explicit linking via `id` and `htmlFor` remains the most robust and standard approach.
**Action:** When a `<label>` cannot be added visibly, always use `aria-label` directly on the `<input>` or `<textarea>`. When visible labels exist, always explicitly link them with `id`/`htmlFor` rather than relying on proximity.
