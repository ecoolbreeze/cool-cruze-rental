## 2023-10-24 - Form Accessibility and Visible Labels\n**Learning:** Relying solely on structural proximity for visible labels or visual placeholders for form fields is insufficient for screen readers. Explicit associations (using `htmlFor` and `id`) or `aria-label` attributes are necessary for proper accessibility.\n**Action:** When adding form inputs, always ensure visible `<label>` elements are explicitly associated with their corresponding inputs using matching `htmlFor` and `id` attributes. If a visible label is absent due to design constraints, use the `aria-label` attribute on the input element.
